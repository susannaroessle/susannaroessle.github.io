Philosophical Coaching · Brussels

I am a philosopher and coach based in Brussels, currently completing my ICF Level 2 accreditation in Transformational Leadership Coaching at TPC Leadership.

My approach draws on a Master's in Philosophy (Freie Universität Berlin, Feminist and Critical Studies) and years of practice managing complex projects across culture, film, and business. I think conceptually — which means I don't just address individual problems in isolation, but situate them within the larger social, structural, and political contexts that shape them.

Coaching with me is not therapy. It is a rigorous, open-ended enquiry into where you are, how you got there, and what you might want otherwise.

Offerings

Free initial conversation

A no-obligation call to explore what you are looking for and whether working together makes sense. We use this time to define scope and format.

Individual sessions

Sessions are tailored to you — online or in person in Brussels. Up to six hours total, taken in one sitting or distributed across multiple meetings. Topics range from personal orientation and life transitions to questions touching on work, identity, and meaning.

Ongoing work

Where needed, we continue beyond the initial sessions. Duration and frequency are agreed together.

I work primarily with women up to 30. All enquiries welcome.
